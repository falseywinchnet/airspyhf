==============================================================================
E11  the two loop edges, measured separately
==============================================================================
Loop gain = A x B, where
  A = client -> device : ms of DEVICE disturbance (ADC loss +
      device discard) caused by X ms of purely client disturbance
  B = device -> client : ms of CLIENT disturbance (host drop + app
      drop) caused by X ms of purely transport disturbance
If either edge is identically zero, no closed loop exists and
recursive manufacture is impossible regardless of the other edge.
